◆ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our logistics and freight forwarding services.

What logistics services does Four Line Logistics provide?

Four Line Logistics provides freight forwarding, sea freight, air freight, road transportation, customs clearance, warehousing, distribution, documentation, project cargo, specialized transportation and supply chain management services.

Do you offer international freight forwarding services?

Yes. We provide international freight forwarding through sea, air and land transportation solutions, supporting businesses moving cargo between Saudi Arabia and international markets.

What types of cargo can you handle?

We support general cargo, commercial goods, industrial equipment, machinery, project cargo, break bulk shipments, retail products and specialized freight for different industries.

Do you provide customs clearance services in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Our customs coordination supports import and export shipments through documentation, customs procedures and applicable requirements.

What are your sea freight services?

Our sea freight services include Full Container Load (FCL), Less than Container Load (LCL), international ocean freight and import-export shipping solutions.

How do I choose the right logistics company in Saudi Arabia?

Consider experience, network coverage, available freight options, customs expertise, communication, shipment coordination and the provider's ability to support your specific cargo requirements.

Can you manage complete supply chain operations?

Our supply chain management services help coordinate transportation and related logistics activities around suppliers, cargo movements, destinations and operational requirements.

How can I get a logistics quote?

You can contact Four Line Logistics by phone, email, WhatsApp or through the request form. Share your origin, destination, cargo type, volume or dimensions and delivery timeline for a suitable logistics assessment.

What documents are required for import and export in Saudi Arabia?

Requirements can include commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, bills of lading or air waybills, customs documentation and other applicable shipment documents.

What are the main shipping routes used by freight forwarders in Saudi Arabia?

Major logistics gateways include Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam and King Abdullah Port. Air and road freight routes also connect Saudi Arabia with regional and international markets.
